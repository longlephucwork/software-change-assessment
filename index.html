      How the score works

      Based on the Equation of Software Design from Code Simplicity: a change is desirable in proportion to its value and in inverse proportion to its effort.

      V = reach × frequency × impact ÷ 12.5, reduced by harm (0–10 scale). Value now is V discounted for slow release; future value is V per quarter across the expected lifetime, bent by the value trend.

      Implementation effort runs 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 from hours to months. Maintenance runs 0.25 to 4 per quarter across the lifetime, bent by its trend. The longer the lifetime, the more the score depends on future value against maintenance, as the book predicts.

      Verdicts: 2 and above Strong, 1 to 2 Worth doing, 0.5 to 1 Marginal, below 0.5 Avoid. Use the score to rank changes against each other, not as an absolute truth.
